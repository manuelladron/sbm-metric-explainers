Benchmark Metric Explainers

The SBM benchmark evaluates generated room layouts on three complementary axes: whether the right inventory is present, whether a person can reach it, and whether the geometry is physically plausible. Each explainer walks through one metric with interactive examples.

Coverage

100 × (Σ wₖsₖ / Σ wₖ − pextra)

Did the method furnish the room with the right inventory? Coverage measures whether required items are present in the right quantities, weighted by importance, with a mild penalty for hallucinated extras. It is deliberately not a geometry metric.

Navigability

100 × (SR − 0.35 × DF)

Can someone walk in from the door and reach the things that matter? Navigability turns that question into pathfinding on a rasterized walkable grid, measuring both reachability and how much furniture forces inconvenient detours.

Overlap & Clearance

0.5 × EOF + 0.2 × GOA + 0.3 × DCI

Is the geometry physically plausible? OC measures furniture collisions, blocked doorway zones, and objects that stick outside the room. It is the cleanup metric that makes bad geometry explicit, even when coverage and navigability look fine.
